Cruise Hub privacy policy

Cruise Hub is an app by Marvel Corp. It is not affiliated with any cruise line. Last updated October 22, 2026.

The short version

Cruise Hub is a cruise planner. It does not sell or book cruises or travel. What you type into Cruise Hub stays on your phone. We have no Cruise Hub account system and no database of your trips. There are no ads, no ad trackers and no analytics in the app. A few features you choose to use send data to other services, and each one is listed below.

What stays on your phone

Your cruises and trips, ship, cabin and booking numbers, port days and all-aboard times, excursions, other bookings (including confirmation numbers and phone numbers you enter), payments, onboard spending and perks, packing and documents lists, trip diary, to-dos, lists and settings are saved in your browser's storage on your phone. We cannot see them. A pasted confirmation email is read on your phone; it is not sent anywhere. If Day Hub is on the same phone, Cruise Hub shows Day Hub's trips (read only), and Day Hub shows Cruise Hub's; that happens on the phone. You can erase everything any time in ⚙ Settings → My data → Erase everything. Erase also signs the phone out of Google, tells Google to drop that sign-in, and forgets saved purchase emails and the AI passphrase for every Hub app on the phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its site data also removes everything.

Google user data

Cruise Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the features you turned on, in the app, on your phone. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and no person at Marvel Corp reads it. To disconnect, use ⚙ Settings in Cruise Hub or remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The backup copy can be deleted from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

Children

Cruise Hub is for people 13 and older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Selling data

We do not sell or rent personal information. We hold almost none of it.

Changes

If what Cruise Hub sends anywhere changes, this page changes first, and the date at the top changes with it.
