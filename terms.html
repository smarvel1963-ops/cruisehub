Your data

Your cruise information lives on your phone, and in your own Google Drive if you turn on backup. You are responsible for keeping a backup. Phones get lost and browsers get cleared, and we cannot recover data we never had.

Check your times, documents and ship information

Cruise Hub keeps what you type in, and what it works out from it (all-aboard reminders, countdowns, packing and document lists) is a guide. Always check sailing times, all-aboard times, port and flight information, and the travel documents you need with your cruise line, airline and the port. Missing the ship is on you, not the app, so don't rely on Cruise Hub alone for an all-aboard time. Maps, weather and place information can be wrong or out of date. AI helper answers can be wrong, so check anything important.
